# BMAD execution adaptation

Scope and authority: this is bounded execution and review guidance for Uroboros. User requirements, project constraints, permissions, reviewer authority, and retained evidence take precedence. It does not authorize hooks, menus, extra seats, or changed approval rules.

Both coworkers may inspect additional authorized code and evidence beyond what the other initially saw. This guidance and shared context are a starting point, not a limit on independent review; existing issue-by-issue questions, evidence citations, and reviewer judgment remain intact.

Adapted from BMAD-METHOD `skills/bmad-correct-course/SKILL.md` at `abe4eb1bce919c9d22cd18b3519353d5824c4b75`.

Keep the approved scope visible while implementing and reviewing. Ask whether a discovery invalidates an approved assumption, changes risk or dependencies, or needs the existing clarification or replan route. State what completed work can remain and what needs fresh evidence. Do not treat a task list or a statement that a method was followed as proof of completion.
